# Next Steps: 3-Way Instructional Capacity & Section Sizing Balance

## 1. Context & Motivation

During executive review with the Dean of the Faculty (DF), the Dean's evaluation of departmental efficiency focused primarily on **"sections per faculty member"** (especially compared to policy targets).

However, as department leadership noted:
> *Evaluating workload strictly by sections per faculty member ignores the fact that departments frequently carry massive section sizes (e.g., 30–50 cadets/section) to cope with instructional shortages. A department teaching 12 oversized sections of 50 cadets appears to be "under-loaded" in raw section counts, yet under standard USAFA classroom caps (≤ 24 cadets), that exact same cohort requires 25 sections—revealing severe under-resourcing.*

To bridge this gap and provide an objective analytical foundation for the Dean, the Workbench needs a **3-Way Capacity & Sizing Balance Metric**.

---

## 2. Mathematical Formulation

For each Academic Department $D$ and Academic School $S$:

### Metric 1: Staffing Capacity ($S_{\text{capacity}}$) — "What Faculty Should Teach"
The total instructional section capacity generated by the department's authorized faculty based on their assigned Role Tiers:
$$S_{\text{capacity}} = \sum_{f \in \text{Faculty}(D)} \text{TargetSections}(f.\text{role})$$
- *Line Faculty*: $3.0$
- *CDs / 306 FTG Flyers*: $2.0$
- *Dept Head / Lab Dir*: $1.0$
- *Adjunct / Chair*: $0.5$
- *MOA / Courtesy*: $0.0$
- *Lab Staff*: $0.0$

### Metric 2: Actual Scheduled ($S_{\text{actual}}$) — "What Is Currently Taught"
The total weighted sections currently scheduled in the timetable:
$$S_{\text{actual}} = \sum_{s \in \text{Sections}(D)} \text{SectionWeight}(s)$$

### Metric 3: Standard Demand ($S_{\text{standard}}$) — "Right-Sized at Standard Cap"
The number of sections that would be required if every course section optimized towards the standard USAFA cap ($C = 24$ cadets) without exceeding it:
$$S_{\text{standard}} = \sum_{c \in \text{Courses}(D)} \left\lceil \frac{\text{Cadets}(c)}{C} \right\rceil \times \text{CourseWeight}(c)$$
*(Excluding 499 Independent Studies, which carry 0.0 section prep).*

---

## 3. Key Analytical Deltas & Insights

### Delta A: Classroom Compression Pressure ($\Delta_{\text{sizing}}$)
$$\Delta_{\text{sizing}} = S_{\text{standard}} - S_{\text{actual}}$$
- **If $\Delta_{\text{sizing}} > 0$ (Classroom Compression)**:
  The department is running oversized sections. They are "masking" an instructional deficit by packing classrooms beyond policy standards.
- **If $\Delta_{\text{sizing}} = 0$ (Balanced Sizing)**:
  Sections are aligned with policy targets ($~24$ cadets/section).
- **If $\Delta_{\text{sizing}} < 0$ (Section Fragmentation)**:
  The department is running small or boutique sections ($< 24$ cadets). Potential for consolidation.

### Delta B: True Faculty Staffing Balance ($\Delta_{\text{staffing}}$)
$$\Delta_{\text{staffing}} = S_{\text{capacity}} - S_{\text{standard}}$$
- **If $\Delta_{\text{staffing}} < 0$ (Faculty Deficit)**:
  Even if the department wanted to right-size its classes to $\le 24$ cadets, its current faculty headcount is mathematically insufficient.
- **If $\Delta_{\text{staffing}} > 0$ (Staffing Surplus)**:
  The department possesses sufficient faculty authorizations to teach standard-sized sections.

## 4. Implementation Plan

### Step 1: Engine Calculations (`dashboard/js/workbench_engine.js`)
Add calculations to the department and school summary functions in `workbench_engine.js`:
- In `computeDepartmentMetrics(deptId)`:
  1. Calculate `targetSections` ($S_{\text{capacity}}$) from the active faculty roster and role tiers.
  2. Calculate `actualSections` ($S_{\text{actual}}$) from course sections.
  3. Calculate `standardSections` ($S_{\text{standard}}$) by iterating over courses, computing `Math.ceil(cadets / 24) * weight` (with 24 pulled from configurable policy).
  4. Compute `compressionDelta = standardSections - actualSections`.
  5. Compute `staffingDelta = targetSections - standardSections`.
- Aggregate these rollups at the School level in `computeSchoolMetrics(schoolId)`.

### Step 2: Department Drilldown UI (`dashboard/js/department_view.js` & `dashboard/index.html`)
Add a new visual card to the Department Drilldown:
- **Card Title**: `⚖️ Instructional Capacity & Section Sizing Balance`
- **Visual Display**:
  - Three comparative metric boxes or a 3-bar comparison chart:
    1. **Staffing Capacity (Target)**: e.g., `24.0 secs`
    2. **Actual Scheduled**: e.g., `18.0 secs`
    3. **Right-Sized Demand (Cap 24)**: e.g., `26.0 secs`
  - **Dynamic Narrative Badge**:
    - If `compressionDelta > 2`: *"⚠️ High Classroom Compression: Department absorbs +X.X sections via oversized classes. Right-sizing to 24 cadets requires X additional sections."*
    - If `compressionDelta < -2`: *"ℹ️ Section Consolidation Potential: Current sections average below 24 cadets (-X.X sections)."*
    - If `staffingDelta < 0`: *"🚨 Structural Faculty Deficit: Staffing capacity (X) is below right-sized demand (Y)."*

### Step 3: School Drilldown Comparison Table
In `dashboard/js/department_view.js` (`renderSchoolDeptsTable`):
- Add columns to the department table:
  - `Target Secs` ($S_{\text{capacity}}$)
  - `Actual Secs` ($S_{\text{actual}}$)
  - `Cap 24 Secs` ($S_{\text{standard}}$)
  - `Compression` ($\Delta_{\text{sizing}}$)

### Step 4: Admin Policy Configuration (`dashboard/js/admin_view.js`)
- Add a configuration field under Policy Settings:
  - `Standard Section Target Cap` (Default: `24`, editable: `15–35`).
- Ensure changes immediately trigger reactive recalculation.
